# Product discovery module

Owner: Jarvis / Bonga Bhengu Omni Route
Purpose: Turn a customer problem into a small concept worth testing.

## Capabilities

- Problem hypothesis
- Concept brief
- Validation questions

## Task contract

Starter task: Sketch one offer or product to test
Deliverable: A named customer problem, a small solution, three validation questions and the smallest prototype. Label demand and pricing assumptions as hypotheses.
Estimated work: 30 minutes.

## Evidence and execution

Use the owner's approved facts and supplied records. Keep missing information unknown. Separate verified sources from hypotheses. Never turn a name match into account ownership, a lead into a customer, or a draft into a published result. For current tools, eligibility, deadlines or market claims, require dated primary-source research. Request research when the data is missing. Generate a draft or task assignment; record completion only after the owner confirms it. Do not send messages, publish, spend money, apply, change profiles or alter CRM.
